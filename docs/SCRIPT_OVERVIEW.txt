VOIS ASSET AUDIT SCRIPT - OVERVIEW
Script version 2026.10.14.11
====================================================================

Use this to review the script and ask for changes. To ask for a change,
name the tab and section, for example:
  "In Stock & Utilisation > By market, add ..."  or  "Add a status to the list".


1. WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES WHEN YOU RUN IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1) Reads the exports
   - Finds the VCAMS export tabs by their headers.
   - Accepts different column names, ignoring case and spaces, e.g. "Asset no." or "Asset Number".
   - Tells the previous export from the current one by the tab name ("Previous" / "Current", or the date in the
     name). Otherwise the older data is the previous export.
   - The raw tabs are never changed.

2) Reconciles the two months (matched on Asset no. only)
   - New assets, removed assets, and every status change.
   - The operational list: assets that became Scrapped or Lost this month.
   - The PO validation you supply, reconciled against the new assets. Nothing is filled in or approved
     automatically.

3) Audits the current export
   - Completeness, validity, duplicates, consistency and timeliness of every record.
   - With a user directory: assets held by people who have left.
   - With an Intune / SCCM export: devices missing from device management, and devices online although VCAMS says
     they are scrapped, in stock or lost.

4) Measures stock and lifecycle
   - Utilisation, stock age, deployments per month, months of cover, idle stock, and where to move stock before
     buying.
   - Over-age and out-of-warranty assets, and the refresh forecast.

5) Compares with the previous audit (from the saved History) and writes a text summary for the Copilot agent.


2. WHAT YOU GET - THE TABS, IN ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1) START HERE
   - How to use the workbook, and the run information: tabs read, columns found, references used.

1b) MONTHLY EMAIL (only when there is a previous month to compare with)
   - The monthly email, ready to copy into Outlook: subject and body. It holds only what the monthly email
     already had: totals, the summary by type, new and removed assets, serial number changes, Scrapped and
     Lost, PO validation, and points for review. No names or emails.
   - A prompt to paste into Copilot in PowerPoint (opened from the VOIS template) that builds a 3-slide deck
     with charts from the same numbers. Kept under 2,000 characters.

2) EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   - The monthly reconciliation (completion report).
   - The quality checks that did not pass.
   - The headline per market and the findings: what we found, impact, action, owner.

3) MONTHLY KPIs
   - Every KPI for the monthly report: this audit, the previous one, the change, and per market.

4) PIVOT & SUMMARY
   - Assets by type: previous month, difference, this month.
   - High value / Low value / Other totals and the grand total, as live formulas with a check column.
   - Status changes by transition.
   - New assets by type, PO and model.
   - The PivotTables NewAssetsPivot, StatusChangesPivot and POValidationsPivot.

5) NEW ASSETS (tblNewAssets)
   - Every new asset, with the 13 standard columns.

6) REMOVED ASSETS (tblRemovedAssets)
   - Only shown when there are any. Each one needs review; none is assumed scrapped.

6b) SERIAL NUMBER CHANGES (tblSerialChanges)
   - Assets in both months whose serial number was changed, added or removed.
   - Case and spacing differences are ignored, and "N/A" counts as blank.

7) SCRAP & INACTIVE ASSETS (tblStatusChanges)
   - Assets whose status changed to Scrapped or Lost this month.

8) POs VALIDATED (tblPOValidated)
   - Your PO rows, exactly as supplied.
   - The PO reconciliation: export count, your quantity, the variance, and the result.
   - The flags for manual review.

9) QUALITY CHECKS
   - Every control (Pass / Review / Fail) and the completion report.

10) INTEGRITY SCORE
   - The share of clean records by market, data-quality dimension, asset type, site and team.

11) EXCEPTIONS
   - The work list: one row per problem, most severe first, with the fix and the owner.

12) DUPLICATES
   - Records sharing an asset no., serial, tag, hostname or MAC.

13) USERS & LEAVERS
   - Assets held by leavers, users holding too many devices, and users not in the directory.

14) RECONCILIATION
   - VCAMS compared with Intune / SCCM: not seen, stale, online but not in use, a different user, and devices
     missing from VCAMS.

15) STOCK & UTILISATION
   - By market and type, and by site.
   - Stock moves to make before buying.
   - In-stock models.
   - Stock by status (in repair / on order).

16) LIFECYCLE
   - The refresh forecast (overdue, due in 0-3, 4-6 and 7-12 months) and the age profile.

17) SINCE LAST AUDIT
   - Each check this audit compared with the previous one, per market.

18) INVENTORY OVERVIEW
   - Counts by stage, type and market.
   - How each VCAMS status and type was read.
   - Top models.
   - Names written several ways.

19) PHYSICAL CHECK
   - A random sample to verify on site. Type Y/N in the yellow columns and run the script again to get the result.

20) ALL ASSETS
   - Every record with its problems. Above 20,000 assets it is not repeated; use the raw tab.

21) GLOSSARY
   - Every check (why it matters, how to fix it, what it needs), the controlled statuses and types, and the Mappings.


3. THE CONTROLLED LISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Statuses and how the audit reads them (any other status is flagged for review):

   In operation                                         in use
   In stock, Reserved, Ready for use, To install,
     Building                                           stock
   Ordered                                              on order
   Disabled (no network for 90+ days),
     Authorised inactive (user on long leave)           inactive
   Scrapped, Marketed (donated to charity)              scrapped / disposed
   Lost                                                 lost

Asset types (any other type is shown as "Unlisted" and flagged):

   High value   Notebook, Desktop, Monitor, Zubehör-Client, Headset
   Low value    Bag, Mouse, Mobile, Keyboard
   Other        IP Phone, VC Device, Display unit, Printer, Wireless sharing device, Connectors, Scanner,
                External Drive, Virtual Machine


4. SETTINGS THAT ARE EASY TO CHANGE (Mappings: Setting | name | value)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Not physically verified: 365 days
   - Not seen (last scan): over 90 days (the same as the VCAMS Disabled rule)
   - Online but scrapped / in stock: seen in the last 30 days, after the status change
   - Aged stock: over 180 days in stock
   - In repair too long: 30 days; ordered / in transit too long: 60 days
   - Overstocked: over 3 months of cover; low stock: under half a month
   - Process-wide problem: on more than 25% of records
   - Expected life: Notebook 4 years, Desktop 5, Monitor 7, Mobile 3, Headset 3 (Mappings: Lifecycle)
   - Devices per user: 1 notebook, 1 desktop, 1 mobile (Mappings: Limit)
   - Physical Check sample: 50 assets
